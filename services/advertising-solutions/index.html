Strategic ATL Campaigns & BTL Activities

Providing companies with our creative inspirations has helped them achieve all their commercial objectives.

Strategic ATL Campaigns

Above-the-line mass media placements that command broad attention and top-of-mind brand recognition:

  • Print Media: National daily newspapers and leading magazines.
  • Electronic Media (Radio): Creative jingles and commercial radio spots.
  • Electronic Media (TV): High-production TV commercials (TVC) broadcast on major networks.
  • Digital & Social Media: Targeted digital campaigns across leading social platforms.

BTL Designs & Activities

Below-the-line targeted outdoor and retail marketing determinants that drive immediate point-of-sale engagement:

  • Hoarding Boards: Prime outdoor billboard placements in strategic Kathmandu and highway corridors.
  • Street Pole Banners: High-frequency street pole banners across key transit routes.
  • POP Materials: Point-of-purchase displays, promotional counters, standees, and retail shelf talkers.
  • Commercial Photography: Professional commercial photography capturing campaigns, products, and activations.
  • Commercial Videography: Broadcast and digital video production, documentation, and editing.
