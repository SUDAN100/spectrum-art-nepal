ABOUT INFINITY ADVERTISING

An Odyssey of Creativity & Strategic Advertising in Kathmandu

WHO WE ARE

Providing organizations with sales-driven inspirations.

Infinity Advertising Pvt. Ltd. operates with a view to provide a better platform to display a precise brand recognition of clients’ products or services.

We deliver valuable advertising solutions to our clients with our original strategic methods and creative thinking, backed up by execution and technical excellence. Providing organizations with our sales-driven inspirations has helped them achieve all their commercial objectives.

Through our careful and innovative sellable creative work, we have inspired brand loyalty, impressed audiences with our original designs, and enhanced existing brand images for companies across Nepal.

HOW WE WORK

A Highly Refined 5-Step Process

At Infinity, we understand that the most effective advertising solutions are derived from a clear understanding of our clients' business, their goals and objectives.

01 — STEP

LISTEN

We begin by looking closely at your objectives and finding out what you are trying to achieve in the marketplace.

02 — STEP

THINK

Next, our in-house team collaborates across all aspects of the project, exploring creative concepts and strategic determinants.

03 — STEP

SOLVE

We evaluate alternative creative pathways and select the most effective, sales-driven solution needed to get you your results.

04 — STEP

EXECUTE

Our refined workflow ensures flawless production, technical excellence, and rigorous quality control within defined parameters.

05 — STEP

LAUNCH

We introduce the creative solution to target audiences across ATL, BTL, and digital touchpoints to spark brand loyalty and action.
